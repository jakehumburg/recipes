*(LaSebi Acasa - YouTube for reference)*

## Ingredients

* 2 leeks *(just white part, not leaves)*
* 3 large carrots *(peeled)*
* 1 green bell pepper
* 4 medium - large potatoes *(peeled)*
* 2 zucchinis
* 2 celery sticks
* Handful of parsley

## Spices / seasonings

* 1 tsp pepper
* 1 tsp paprika
* Olive oil
* 1 tbsp salt *(to taste)*

## Instructions

1. Cut all veggies in large chunks.
2. Put in a big pot.
3. Cover with water.
4. Boil for **30-45 min**, until carrot becomes mushy.
5. Remove **half of the water** *(jumătate)*.
6. "Olivia added" **lovage (1 tbsp)**, then add **spices + oil**.
7. Use an **immersion blender** to break everything down.
8. *If too thick, add some water.*

## Croutons (add after / "post")

* Toss bread with: **olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder**
* Mix spices with bread gently.
* Bake **375°F for 10 min**, **spread out** on parchment paper.
