## Ingredients

* Water *(half a pot)*
* Yellow cornmeal *(1.5 cups?)*

## Spices / seasonings

* Salt *("cover the bottom in salt")*
* 1-2 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Boil water on **high**.
2. **Cover the bottom with salt**.
3. Add **1-2 tbsp olive oil**.
4. Bring to a boil.
5. **Whisk quickly all while adding cornmeal**.
6. Keep whisking until you **feel it thicken**.
7. **Turn heat down low**.
8. Keep going with the whisking motion **until clumps are gone**.
9. Let it cool, but **shake/stir so the bottom doesn’t stick**.
10. Pour onto serving plate/bowl.
