*(note: "no metal tools")*

## Ingredients

* Eggplants *(cooked/roasted - whiter core is tastier)*
* Small Onion *(diced small)*

## Spices / seasonings

* Salt *(1 tsp at a time)* + oil

## Instructions

1. **Set oven to 350°F** or **Broil (Hi)**.
2. Use **3-4 eggplants**; **poke with a fork** so they don’t explode *(~5 times, all the way through)*.
3. Roast/broil until the **outside starts to look shiny/hard**, then **flip** and cook **a bit longer** *(another ~30 mins). It will be green inside.
4. **Take out and peel skin**.
5. **Strain/drain the inside** (eggplant flesh).
6. Put eggplant on a surface and **dice/chop with a spatula**.
7. Then use a **mixer** *("make it like a paste")*.
8. **Add oil + spices** **little by little**.

## Onion prep

1. Dice onion and **put in water for 10-20 min**, then strain and dry.
2. Put onion in the **bowl of mixer** (to combine).

## Serve / storage

* **Serve with small bread as a topping**.
* **Can freeze** in a bag **without air**.
